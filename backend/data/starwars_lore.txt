# LOREMIND CORPUS — STAR WARS COMPLETE LORE
# ===========================================

# =====================
# THE FORCE
# =====================

The Force is an energy field created by all living things that binds the galaxy together.
The Force connects all living beings throughout the galaxy and flows through every living creature.
The light side of the Force was associated with peace, knowledge, serenity, and harmony.
The dark side of the Force was fueled by fear, anger, hatred, and suffering.
The Jedi Code emphasized peace, discipline, and detachment from personal emotion.
The Sith Code embraced passion, strength, power, and victory through the dark side.
Balance in the Force was interpreted differently by Jedi and Sith traditions throughout history.
Force-sensitive beings could feel the Force more strongly than ordinary individuals.
Midi-chlorians were microscopic life forms that communicated the will of the Force to Force-sensitive beings.
High midi-chlorian counts indicated strong Force potential in an individual.
Anakin Skywalker had the highest midi-chlorian count ever recorded, surpassing even Master Yoda.
The Jedi believed the Force had a will of its own, guiding events across the galaxy.
Force visions allowed sensitive individuals to glimpse past, present, and possible future events.
Force bonds could form between individuals, connecting them across vast distances.
The dyad in the Force was an extremely rare phenomenon linking two beings as one in the Force.
Rey and Ben Solo formed a Force dyad, the first in generations.
Force ghosts were the spirits of deceased Jedi who had learned to preserve their consciousness after death.
Qui-Gon Jinn was the first Jedi to discover the path to becoming a Force ghost.
Obi-Wan Kenobi, Yoda, and Anakin Skywalker all appeared as Force ghosts after their deaths.
The Cosmic Force encompassed all life and existed beyond death, connecting to the Living Force.
The Living Force referred to the energy of living beings in the present moment.
Force healing allowed certain gifted individuals to mend wounds and even restore life.
Rey and Ben Solo both demonstrated the rare ability to heal using the Force.
Force lightning was a dark side ability used by Sith to channel electricity through their hands.
Emperor Palpatine was particularly skilled with Force lightning, using it as a weapon and execution method.
Force choke allowed dark side users to strangle enemies from a distance using the Force.
Darth Vader frequently used Force choke against subordinates who failed him.
Telekinesis allowed Force users to move objects with their minds.
Force push and Force pull were common telekinetic applications used by both Jedi and Sith.
Force speed allowed users to move at extraordinary velocity for brief periods.
Mind tricks allowed Jedi to influence the thoughts of weak-minded individuals.
Force sense allowed practitioners to feel the emotions, presence, and intentions of others.
Force concealment allowed skilled users to hide their Force presence from detection.
Darth Maul, Yoda, and other Force users could leap extraordinary distances using the Force.
Force absorption allowed certain individuals to absorb and redirect energy attacks.
The Darksider ability known as Force drain could sap the life energy from living beings.
Darth Nihilus was the most extreme practitioner of Force drain, consuming entire planets.
Force stasis could freeze individuals or objects in place temporarily.
Tutaminis was the Jedi art of energy absorption, allowing practitioners to catch blaster bolts.
Force meld allowed a group of Force users to combine their perceptions and fight as one mind.
Niman was a lightsaber form that incorporated Force powers heavily into combat.
The Mortis gods represented different fundamental aspects of the Force.
The Father sought to maintain balance between the Daughter and the Son on Mortis.
The Son embodied the dark side while the Daughter embodied the light side of the Force.
The World Between Worlds existed as a mysterious dimension connected to time and the Force.
Ezra Bridger accessed the World Between Worlds through an ancient Jedi temple on Lothal.
Palpatine sought to control the World Between Worlds to gain power over all of time.
The Chosen One prophecy foretold that a powerful being would bring balance to the Force.
Most Jedi believed Anakin Skywalker was the Chosen One destined to destroy the Sith.
Some interpreted the prophecy to mean the Sith would be destroyed, not necessarily permanently.
Anakin ultimately fulfilled the Chosen One prophecy by destroying Palpatine and bringing balance.
The Force called to individuals in different ways, sometimes compelling them toward certain destinies.
Certain locations in the galaxy were strong in the Force due to ancient events or natural properties.
The planet Ilum was strong in the Force and home to the kyber crystals used in Jedi lightsabers.
Ahch-To was the location of the first Jedi Temple and was deeply connected to the Force.
Exegol was a hidden Sith world strong in the dark side, home to ancient Sith secrets.
The cave on Dagobah was a place where the dark side was particularly concentrated.
Luke Skywalker entered the dark side cave on Dagobah and saw a vision of Darth Vader.
Force nexuses were locations where the Force was unusually strong due to historical events.
The Holocron of Prophecy contained various Force prophecies recorded by ancient Jedi.
Jedi Holocrons were pyramid-shaped data storage devices that could only be opened by Jedi.
Sith Holocrons were more angular in design and could only be accessed by those sensitive to the dark side.
Combining a Jedi and Sith Holocron could unlock secrets of the Force unavailable to either alone.

# =====================
# THE JEDI ORDER
# =====================

The Jedi Order served as guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy for over a thousand generations.
The Jedi Temple on Coruscant was the headquarters of the Jedi Order for millennia.
The Jedi High Council was the governing body of the Jedi Order, consisting of twelve members.
Yoda served on the Jedi High Council for centuries and was its most respected member.
Mace Windu served as one of the most senior members of the Jedi Council alongside Yoda.
Ki-Adi-Mundi, Kit Fisto, Shaak Ti, and Plo Koon were among the Jedi Council members during the Clone Wars.
Jedi younglings began their training in the Force from infancy or early childhood.
The Gathering was a rite of passage where younglings traveled to Ilum to find their kyber crystal.
After finding their kyber crystal, younglings constructed their first lightsabers under the guidance of Professor Huyang.
Padawan learners were apprenticed to Jedi Knights or Masters for further one-on-one training.
Padawans traditionally wore a single braid of hair behind their right ear to signify their status.
The Jedi Trials were a series of tests that Padawans had to pass to become Jedi Knights.
The Trials included tests of skill, courage, spirit, flesh, and insight.
After passing the Trials, a Padawan's braid was cut and they were elevated to the rank of Jedi Knight.
Jedi Masters achieved their rank through great achievement, wisdom, or by successfully training a Padawan to Knighthood.
The rank of Grand Master was reserved for the most senior and respected Jedi leader.
Yoda held the title of Grand Master of the Jedi Order for centuries.
Jedi were forbidden from forming personal attachments, including romantic relationships.
This prohibition on attachment was one of the central tensions within the Jedi Order's philosophy.
Anakin Skywalker's secret marriage to Padmé Amidala violated the Jedi Code directly.
Critics argued the Jedi prohibition on attachment led to emotional repression and vulnerability.
Qui-Gon Jinn was known for following the Living Force rather than strict Jedi doctrine.
Qui-Gon frequently clashed with the Jedi Council over his unconventional approach.
The Jedi Order maintained a strict non-attachment policy toward possessions and wealth.
Jedi were known to carry very few personal belongings, with their lightsaber being their most prized possession.
The lightsaber was described as the weapon of a Jedi Knight, an elegant weapon for a more civilized age.
Jedi served as peacekeepers and diplomats as much as warriors throughout their history.
The Jedi Archives on Coruscant contained one of the most extensive collections of knowledge in the galaxy.
Jedi Consulars focused on diplomacy, knowledge, and the Force over combat.
Jedi Guardians focused on combat training and the physical aspects of the Jedi arts.
Jedi Sentinels balanced combat skills with practical knowledge of the galaxy.
The Jedi agricultural corps provided a path for Force-sensitives who failed to become Padawans.
The Jedi service corps included the agricultural corps, medical corps, exploration corps, and educational corps.
Jedi Shadow was a specialized role responsible for hunting dark side artifacts and practitioners.
Jedi Temple Guards wore white helmets and robes and carried yellow-bladed lightsaber pikes.
The Jedi Order maintained a vast network of temples and outposts across the galaxy.
The Jedi Praxeum on Ossus was an ancient center of Jedi learning destroyed in the Great Sith War.
Luke Skywalker later established a new Jedi temple on Ossus in the New Republic era.
The Jedi Order traced its origins back thousands of years to early Force users who studied the Force.
A schism in the early Jedi Order led to the creation of the Sith as a separate dark side tradition.
The Hundred-Year Darkness was the conflict that drove the proto-Sith from the Jedi Order.
The Jedi Code stated: There is no emotion, there is peace. There is no ignorance, there is knowledge. There is no passion, there is serenity. There is no chaos, there is harmony. There is no death, there is the Force.
Jedi were encouraged to acknowledge emotions but not be controlled by them.
The Jedi Order's refusal to grant Anakin the rank of Master while placing him on the Council created resentment.
Jedi General rank was granted to senior Jedi during the Clone Wars to command Republic forces.
Many Jedi were killed during Order 66, with only a handful surviving across the galaxy.
Surviving Jedi after Order 66 included Obi-Wan Kenobi, Yoda, Ahsoka Tano, Kanan Jarrus, and Cal Kestis.
Jocasta Nu was the Chief Librarian of the Jedi Archives and a fierce defender of Jedi knowledge.
Coleman Trebor was a Jedi Master killed by Jango Fett during the Battle of Geonosis.
Aayla Secura was a Twi'lek Jedi Master killed by clone troopers during Order 66.
Depa Billaba was a Jedi Master who sacrificed herself to save her Padawan Caleb Dume during Order 66.
Caleb Dume changed his name to Kanan Jarrus and hid his Jedi identity after surviving Order 66.
The Inquisitorius was created by Darth Vader and Palpatine to hunt down surviving Jedi after Order 66.
Imperial Inquisitors were former Jedi or Force-sensitives who were turned to the dark side through torture.
The Grand Inquisitor was the leader of the Inquisitorius and had once been a Jedi Temple Guard.
The Second Sister, Trilla Suduri, was a former Padawan who became an Inquisitor after Order 66.
Cal Kestis defeated the Second Sister and helped her remember her Jedi identity before her death.
Reva Sevander, the Third Sister, secretly sought revenge against Darth Vader for his betrayal during Order 66.

# =====================
# THE SITH ORDER
# =====================

The Sith were an ancient order of Force users who embraced the dark side of the Force.
The original Sith were a species native to the planet Moraband, also called Korriban.
Dark Jedi exiled from the Jedi Order discovered the Sith species and became their rulers.
Over centuries, the term Sith came to refer to the dark side order rather than the species.
The Rule of Two was established by Darth Bane after the destruction of the Brotherhood of Darkness.
Darth Bane created the Rule of Two after believing the Sith destroyed themselves through infighting.
The Rule of Two stated there could only be two Sith at any time — a master and an apprentice.
The apprentice was expected to eventually kill the master and take on their own apprentice.
This created a cycle of ever-increasing strength as weaker Sith were eliminated.
Darth Plagueis was a Muun Sith Lord who studied the midi-chlorians and the nature of life and death.
Plagueis claimed to have discovered how to influence midi-chlorians to prevent death.
Palpatine claimed to his apprentice Darth Maul that Plagueis could save those he loved from dying.
Palpatine murdered Darth Plagueis in his sleep after learning everything he could from his master.
Darth Sidious was Palpatine's Sith identity, maintained in secret for decades.
Darth Maul was the first known apprentice of Darth Sidious, a Zabrak warrior raised from childhood as a Sith.
Darth Maul was seemingly killed by Obi-Wan Kenobi on Naboo after bisecting him with a lightsaber.
Maul survived through hatred and dark side energy, driven mad on the junk moon Lotho Minor.
Mother Talzin of the Nightsisters restored Darth Maul with a new set of cybernetic legs.
Maul briefly ruled Mandalore through criminal alliances before being captured by Darth Sidious.
Maul was eventually killed by Obi-Wan Kenobi on Tatooine, fulfilling a decades-long rivalry.
Count Dooku was a Jedi Master who left the Order and became Darth Tyranus, apprentice to Darth Sidious.
Dooku was disillusioned with the Galactic Republic's corruption and sought to reform it through the Confederacy.
As Darth Tyranus, Dooku secretly created the Separatist movement under Palpatine's direction.
Count Dooku recruited General Grievous, Nute Gunray, and other leaders to the Separatist cause.
Dooku was killed by Anakin Skywalker aboard the Invisible Hand at Palpatine's urging.
Anakin's execution of the defenseless Dooku was his first significant step toward the dark side.
Darth Vader became Palpatine's final apprentice after Anakin Skywalker's fall to the dark side.
Palpatine manipulated Anakin's fear of losing Padmé to turn him to the dark side.
The Sith traditionally wore dark robes and used red lightsabers created through kyber crystal bleeding.
Sith holocrons contained dark side teachings and could only be unlocked by dark side users.
The Sith Rule of Two remained secret for nearly a thousand years before Palpatine revealed the Sith's existence.
Yoda famously declared Always two there are — no more, no less — a master and an apprentice.
The Sith Eternal cult on Exegol worshipped the Sith and worked in secret to restore Sith dominance.
Palpatine used the Sith Eternal to construct a fleet of Star Destroyers equipped with planet-killing weapons.
Snoke was a strand-cast created by Palpatine on Exegol to serve as a proxy ruler.
The Final Order was Palpatine's contingency plan for eternal Sith rule over the galaxy.
Ancient Sith Lords included Darth Revan, Darth Malak, Darth Nihilus, Darth Sion, and Darth Traya.
The Sith homeworld of Moraband was visited by Yoda during the Clone Wars in a powerful Force vision.
Darth Krayt founded a new Sith Order called the One Sith in the Legacy era, rejecting the Rule of Two.

# =====================
# ANAKIN SKYWALKER / DARTH VADER
# =====================

Anakin Skywalker was born on Tatooine to Shmi Skywalker, with no father — a virgin birth in the Force.
Many Jedi believed Anakin was conceived by the midi-chlorians themselves.
Qui-Gon Jinn discovered Anakin as a nine-year-old slave on Tatooine.
Qui-Gon believed Anakin was the Chosen One destined to bring balance to the Force.
Anakin demonstrated extraordinary Force ability, reflexes, and piloting skill even as a child.
Anakin built the protocol droid C-3PO from salvaged parts as a child on Tatooine.
Watto was the Toydarian junk dealer who owned both Anakin and his mother Shmi.
Qui-Gon won Anakin's freedom through a podrace bet with Watto.
Anakin competed in the Boonta Eve Classic podrace and won despite his young age.
Shmi Skywalker remained a slave on Tatooine after Anakin left, unable to be freed.
Anakin's attachment to his mother was a source of great emotional conflict throughout his youth.
Shmi Skywalker was later purchased and freed by moisture farmer Cliegg Lars, whom she married.
Shmi was captured by Tusken Raiders and died in Anakin's arms after weeks of captivity and torture.
Anakin massacred the entire Tusken Raider tribe including women and children in revenge for Shmi's death.
This massacre foreshadowed Anakin's capacity for extreme violence when his emotions overrode his Jedi training.
Obi-Wan Kenobi took Anakin as his Padawan after Qui-Gon's death, against the Council's initial wishes.
Anakin secretly married Padmé Amidala on Naboo following the Battle of Geonosis.
Anakin and Padmé married in secret because Jedi were forbidden from forming romantic attachments.
Anakin lost his right arm during the Battle of Geonosis and received a cybernetic replacement.
Anakin became one of the most skilled duelists and pilots in the Jedi Order during the Clone Wars.
Anakin's nickname was The Hero With No Fear, a title earned through extraordinary battlefield exploits.
Anakin formed a close friendship with Chancellor Palpatine, who secretly mentored him toward the dark side.
Palpatine secretly manipulated Anakin's fears about losing Padmé Amidala.
Anakin had premonitions of Padmé dying in childbirth and became obsessed with preventing her death.
Palpatine told Anakin the legend of Darth Plagueis and his power to prevent death.
Anakin interrupted Mace Windu's arrest of Palpatine, allowing Palpatine to kill the Jedi Master.
Immediately after, Anakin pledged himself to Palpatine and became Darth Vader.
As his first act as Darth Vader, Anakin led clone troopers in an assault on the Jedi Temple called Operation Knightfall.
Darth Vader killed dozens of Jedi including Padawans and younglings during Operation Knightfall.
Darth Vader traveled to Mustafar and executed the Separatist leaders as ordered by Palpatine.
Obi-Wan Kenobi followed Padmé to Mustafar and confronted Anakin after his fall to the dark side.
Obi-Wan and Anakin fought a legendary duel on the volcanic world of Mustafar.
Obi-Wan won the duel by taking the high ground and severing both of Anakin's legs and his remaining arm.
Anakin caught fire near the lava banks of Mustafar and suffered catastrophic burns.
Palpatine rescued Anakin and placed him in a life-sustaining suit of black armor.
Darth Vader's suit required constant maintenance and caused him chronic pain throughout his life.
Vader's mask generated a controlled breathing apparatus necessary after his lungs were destroyed by the flames.
Padmé Amidala died shortly after giving birth to twins Luke and Leia, losing the will to live.
Vader believed he had killed Padmé through his Force choke on Mustafar.
Palpatine told Vader he had killed Padmé in his anger, deepening his despair and dependency on the dark side.
Darth Vader became the Emperor's enforcer, hunting down surviving Jedi and crushing Rebel resistance.
Vader commanded the Imperial fleet and was directly beneath only Emperor Palpatine in authority.
Vader constructed a personal castle on Mustafar as a place to meditate and maintain his connection to the dark side.
Vader's fortress on Mustafar was designed by architect Screaming Citadel and built over a Sith cave.
Vader interrogated Princess Leia Organa when she was captured by the Empire over Tatooine.
Vader destroyed Alderaan on Grand Moff Tarkin's order to demonstrate the Death Star's power.
Vader sensed Luke Skywalker's Force presence during the Battle of Yavin.
Vader recognized that the Force was strong with the pilot who destroyed the Death Star.
Vader later confirmed that Luke Skywalker was his son through the Force.
Vader sought to turn Luke to the dark side and rule the galaxy together as father and son.
During the Battle of Cloud City, Vader cut off Luke's right hand and revealed he was Luke's father.
Vader told the Emperor about Luke's existence and arranged for his capture aboard the second Death Star.
During the Emperor's assault on Luke, Vader experienced the remnants of his love for his son.
Vader threw Palpatine into the Death Star's reactor shaft, sacrificing himself to save Luke.
Anakin Skywalker died reconciled to the light side, asking Luke to tell Leia that there was still good in him.
Luke removed Vader's mask before his death so father and son could see each other with their own eyes.
Anakin Skywalker appeared as a Force ghost beside Obi-Wan and Yoda after his death.

# =====================
# OBI-WAN KENOBI
# =====================

Obi-Wan Kenobi was born on Stewjon and showed Force sensitivity from an early age.
Obi-Wan was taken by the Jedi Order as an infant and trained as a Jedi.
Qui-Gon Jinn was Obi-Wan's Jedi Master, teaching him to follow the Living Force.
Obi-Wan initially struggled to connect with the Force on an intuitive level, preferring study and training.
Obi-Wan defeated Darth Maul on Naboo after Maul killed Qui-Gon Jinn.
Obi-Wan used Qui-Gon's lightsaber to cut Darth Maul in half during their duel on Theed.
Obi-Wan trained Anakin Skywalker as his Padawan following Qui-Gon's dying request.
Obi-Wan and Anakin developed a deep brotherly bond during their years together in the Clone Wars.
Obi-Wan was known for his calm demeanor, wit, dry humor, and masterful use of Soresu defensive lightsaber form.
Soresu was a purely defensive lightsaber form focused on economy of movement and redirecting enemy attacks.
Obi-Wan was regarded as one of the greatest Soresu practitioners of his era.
Obi-Wan negotiated with the Kaminoans on Kamino and discovered the secret clone army.
Obi-Wan was captured by Count Dooku on Geonosis, triggering the opening battle of the Clone Wars.
Obi-Wan dueled Darth Maul again on Mandalore during the Siege of Mandalore at the end of the Clone Wars.
Obi-Wan escaped Order 66 with the help of Commander Cody's hesitation and his own quick thinking.
Obi-Wan and Yoda returned to the Jedi Temple and altered the beacon to warn surviving Jedi not to return.
Obi-Wan confronted Anakin on Mustafar after learning of his fall to the dark side from Yoda.
Obi-Wan told Anakin he was his brother and that he loved him before leaving him to burn on Mustafar.
Obi-Wan took baby Luke to Tatooine and left him with his uncle Owen and aunt Beru Lars.
Obi-Wan lived in exile on Tatooine for nearly two decades, watching over Luke from a distance.
Obi-Wan learned from Qui-Gon's Force ghost how to preserve his consciousness after death during exile.
During his exile, Obi-Wan was drawn into a confrontation with Darth Vader involving a young Leia Organa.
Obi-Wan encountered Darth Vader for the first time since Mustafar during this mission to rescue Leia.
Obi-Wan eventually escaped and returned to Tatooine, continuing his watch over Luke.
Obi-Wan left Tatooine with Luke Skywalker in response to Princess Leia's distress message.
Obi-Wan told Luke about his father, describing Vader as a pupil who betrayed and murdered Anakin Skywalker.
Obi-Wan sacrificed himself aboard the Death Star, allowing Darth Vader to strike him down.
Obi-Wan told Vader that if he struck him down, he would become more powerful than Vader could imagine.
Obi-Wan vanished instead of dying conventionally, becoming one with the Force.
Obi-Wan guided Luke as a Force ghost during the Battle of Yavin, telling him to use the Force.
Obi-Wan appeared to Luke on Hoth and directed him to go to Dagobah to train with Yoda.
Obi-Wan's Force ghost appeared alongside Yoda and Anakin Skywalker at the celebration on Endor.

# =====================
# LUKE SKYWALKER
# =====================

Luke Skywalker was born on Polis Massa and immediately separated from his twin sister Leia after Padmé's death.
Luke was taken to Tatooine by Obi-Wan Kenobi and left with his uncle Owen Lars and aunt Beru.
Luke grew up on Tatooine dreaming of adventure and joining the Academy like his friends.
Owen Lars was Anakin Skywalker's stepbrother, son of Cliegg Lars and his relationship with Shmi.
Luke received R2-D2 and C-3PO when his uncle purchased them from Jawas in the desert.
R2-D2 secretly carried Princess Leia's message and the stolen Death Star plans.
Luke discovered Obi-Wan Kenobi living in the Jundland Wastes of Tatooine after following R2-D2.
Imperial stormtroopers killed Owen and Beru Lars, destroying Luke's home and reason to stay.
Luke traveled with Obi-Wan to Mos Eisley spaceport to find passage to Alderaan.
Luke first met Han Solo and Chewbacca at the Mos Eisley Cantina.
Han Solo piloted the Millennium Falcon and agreed to take Luke, Obi-Wan, and the droids to Alderaan.
Luke witnessed Alderaan's destruction by the Death Star as they arrived at its location.
Luke was captured along with Han and Chewbacca aboard the Death Star.
Luke rescued Princess Leia from the Death Star's detention block, sparking the Galactic Civil War.
Luke destroyed the Death Star during the Battle of Yavin using the Force to guide his proton torpedo.
Luke received Jedi training from Yoda on Dagobah after being directed there by Obi-Wan's Force ghost.
Yoda was reluctant to train Luke, believing he was too old and too full of recklessness.
Luke left Dagobah before completing his training to try to save his friends on Cloud City.
Darth Vader cut off Luke's right hand during their duel in Cloud City and revealed he was his father.
Luke refused to join Vader and fell into a processing vent rather than submit to the dark side.
Luke received a cybernetic replacement hand after losing his right hand to Vader.
Luke returned to Dagobah to complete his training but found Yoda dying of old age.
Yoda told Luke there is another Skywalker when Luke said only Vader remained for him to face.
Luke learned from Obi-Wan's ghost that Leia was his twin sister.
Luke surrendered to Imperial forces and was brought before Emperor Palpatine aboard the second Death Star.
Luke refused to turn to the dark side and appeal the Emperor's corruption.
Luke attacked Vader with the full force of his anger but stopped himself from killing his father.
Luke declared he was a Jedi like his father before him and threw away his lightsaber.
Luke's faith in his father's goodness triggered Vader's redemption and the Emperor's death.
Luke was the last known Jedi Master in the galaxy for decades following the fall of the Empire.
Luke established a new Jedi Order and took on students including his nephew Ben Solo.
Luke sensed the darkness growing in Ben and briefly ignited his lightsaber before stopping himself.
Luke's moment of fear and doubt contributed to Ben Solo turning to the dark side as Kylo Ren.
Overcome by guilt, Luke exiled himself to Ahch-To, the site of the first Jedi Temple.
Rey tracked Luke down using a map assembled from fragments scattered across the galaxy.
Luke initially refused to train Rey but eventually began teaching her about the Force.
Luke died on Ahch-To after projecting his Force presence across the galaxy to Crait to save the Resistance.
Luke's Force projection was so intense and unprecedented that it consumed his physical body.
Luke became one with the Force on Ahch-To, his body disappearing as he reached out through the Force.
Luke appeared as a Force ghost to Rey during her confrontation with Palpatine on Exegol.

# =====================
# LEIA ORGANA
# =====================

Leia Organa was born on Polis Massa as the twin sister of Luke Skywalker.
Leia was adopted by Bail Organa and Queen Breha Organa of Alderaan immediately after her birth.
Leia secretly possessed strong Force sensitivity as the daughter of Anakin Skywalker.
Leia grew up as a princess of Alderaan and followed her father into the Senate.
Leia became the youngest Senator in the Galactic Senate's history at age sixteen.
Leia led a double life as a loyal Imperial Senator while secretly aiding the Rebel Alliance.
Leia stored the Death Star plans inside R2-D2 and sent him to find Obi-Wan Kenobi on Tatooine.
Leia was captured by Darth Vader while aboard the Tantive IV over Tatooine.
Leia was tortured aboard the Death Star but refused to reveal the location of the Rebel base.
Leia was forced to watch the destruction of her home planet Alderaan by the Death Star.
Alderaan's destruction killed billions of people and was one of the Empire's greatest atrocities.
Leia helped Luke and Han escape the Death Star and returned to the Rebel Alliance.
Leia awarded medals to Luke Skywalker and Han Solo after the Battle of Yavin.
Leia served as a general and key leader of the Rebel Alliance throughout the Galactic Civil War.
Leia received a vision of her mother through the Force, though she had no conscious memories of her.
Leia married Han Solo after the Battle of Endor and they had a son, Ben Solo.
Leia sensed Ben Solo turning to the dark side and was unable to stop it.
Leia trained briefly as a Jedi under Luke Skywalker but stopped after sensing her own path led to her death.
Leia led the Resistance against the First Order in the absence of a New Republic military response.
Leia used the Force for the first time in combat to survive in the vacuum of space after an assassination attempt.
Leia passed away peacefully, sending the last of her strength to reach Ben Solo through the Force.
Ben Solo felt his mother's death through their Force bond and was momentarily distracted in his fight with Rey.
Leia's last act was calling out to her son across the galaxy and guiding him back to the light.

# =====================
# HAN SOLO
# =====================

Han Solo grew up as an orphan on Corellia, running with street gangs controlled by the White Worms.
Han met Qi'ra on Corellia and they planned to escape together using stolen coaxium fuel.
Han escaped the White Worms but Qi'ra was captured, separating them.
Han joined the Imperial Navy as a flight cadet to earn passage off Corellia.
Han was dishonorably discharged from the Imperial infantry after three years for insubordination.
Han met Tobias Beckett and his crew of criminals while serving as an Imperial grunt on Mimban.
Han joined Beckett's crew in a heist to steal a shipment of unrefined coaxium.
The heist on Vandor went wrong when the Cloud Riders, led by Enfys Nest, attacked them.
Han met Chewbacca in an Imperial prison pit where Han was thrown to be eaten by the Wookiee.
Han and Chewbacca formed a lifelong friendship and partnership after Han spoke a few words of Shyriiwook.
Han won the Millennium Falcon from Lando Calrissian in a sabacc game, though Lando disputed the result.
Han made the Kessel Run in less than twelve parsecs using a hyperspace route near a black hole cluster.
Han became a smuggler and worked extensively for Jabba the Hutt, often running spice.
Han dropped a shipment of spice when boarded by Imperial customs, leading Jabba to put a bounty on him.
Han helped Luke and Obi-Wan escape Tatooine and was initially motivated purely by the reward money.
Han returned at the last moment during the Battle of Yavin to clear Vader's shot at Luke.
Han's rescue of Luke at Yavin became one of the most celebrated acts of the Galactic Civil War.
Han and Leia developed a romantic relationship despite constant bickering and tension.
Han was captured by Boba Fett and delivered to Jabba the Hutt, frozen in carbonite.
Leia rescued Han from Jabba's palace on Tatooine, disguised as the bounty hunter Boushh.
Han was promoted to General and led the ground assault on the second Death Star's shield generator on Endor.
Han and Leia married after the Battle of Endor and had a son named Ben.
Han struggled with parenthood and spent much of his time smuggling again after Ben turned to the dark side.
Han was reunited with Rey, Finn, and BB-8 when they accidentally activated the Millennium Falcon's tracker.
Han confronted his son Kylo Ren on the Starkiller Base and called him by his birth name, Ben.
Kylo Ren killed Han Solo by plunging his lightsaber through his father's chest on Starkiller Base.
Han's death devastated the Resistance and permanently scarred both Leia and his son Ben.

# =====================
# YODA
# =====================

Yoda was a member of an unnamed species known for extraordinary Force sensitivity and longevity.
Yoda was nearly nine hundred years old at the time of his death on Dagobah.
Yoda had trained Jedi for eight hundred years by the time of the Clone Wars.
Yoda was the Grand Master of the Jedi Order and the most respected Jedi in history.
Yoda was incredibly powerful with the Force, capable of feats few other Jedi could match.
Yoda defeated Count Dooku in a lightsaber duel on Geonosis but could not capture him.
Yoda dueled Darth Sidious directly in the Senate chamber, but neither could decisively defeat the other.
Yoda escaped Coruscant after the duel with Sidious by hiding in Bail Organa's escape pod.
Yoda went into exile on the swamp planet Dagobah after the fall of the Republic.

The clone army was secretly commissioned by Sifo-Dyas, a Jedi who foresaw war coming.
Sifo-Dyas was murdered by Count Dooku, who then secretly redirected the project for the Sith.
The Kaminoans engineered the clones on the ocean planet Kamino over a period of ten years.
Clone troopers were grown at twice the normal rate of human development.
The clones were given inhibitor chips that would activate Order 66 on Palpatine's command.
Jango Fett requested that one unmodified clone be raised as his son, resulting in Boba Fett.
Boba Fett watched his father be killed by Mace Windu during the Battle of Geonosis.
Boba Fett vowed revenge against Mace Windu and spent years pursuing that goal.
General Grievous was a Kaleesh warrior who volunteered to have his body rebuilt as a cyborg general.
Grievous trained himself in lightsaber combat and collected the weapons of defeated Jedi as trophies.
Grievous could wield four lightsabers simultaneously using his four mechanical arms.
The Separatist droid army was composed primarily of B1 battle droids, B2 super battle droids, and droideka destroyer droids.

Order 66 was a secret command implanted into clone troopers through inhibitor chips in their brains.

Most Jedi were killed within minutes of Order 66's activation, with only a handful escaping.
The Siege of Mandalore took place at the same time as Order 66 and Palpatine's rise to Emperor.
Ahsoka Tano and Rex survived Order 66 together on Mandalore after Rex removed his inhibitor chip.
Ahsoka staged her own death to escape Imperial pursuit after Order 66.

# =====================

The Galactic Empire replaced the Republic following Palpatine's declaration of himself as Emperor.
Palpatine's declaration transformed the democratic Senate into an authoritarian imperial structure.
The Empire maintained control through fear, military occupation, and widespread propaganda.
The Imperial military consisted of stormtroopers, star destroyers, TIE fighters, and planetary garrisons.
Stormtroopers replaced clone troopers as the primary soldiers of the Empire within years of its formation.
Imperial stormtroopers were recruited from human volunteers and conscripted populations across the galaxy.
Moff was an Imperial rank given to regional governors responsible for sectors of the Empire.
Grand Moff was a higher rank given to those overseeing priority sectors of particular importance.
Grand Moff Tarkin was one of the most senior Imperial commanders and a close ally of Palpatine.
Tarkin formulated the Tarkin Doctrine, which held that fear of force was more effective than force itself.
The Tarkin Doctrine directly led to the construction of the Death Star as a symbol of terror.
The Death Star was designed as a superweapon capable of destroying entire planets with a single shot.
The Death Star was decades in the making, with its designs originating during the Clone Wars.
The Death Star's primary weapon focused kyber crystal energy through an amplification system.
Galen Erso was the chief engineer of the Death Star and secretly designed a thermal exhaust flaw.
The thermal exhaust port flaw allowed a single proton torpedo to trigger a chain reaction destroying the station.
Galen Erso sent a message to Saw Gerrera revealing the flaw through defecting pilot Bodhi Rook.
The Rebel Alliance's Rogue One team stole the Death Star plans from the Imperial facility on Scarif.
All members of Rogue One died in the mission, transmitting the plans to a Rebel corvette at the last moment.
The second Death Star was larger than the first and had its primary weapon operational before completion.
The Emperor used the second Death Star as bait to lure the Rebel fleet into a trap.
Moff Gideon commanded Imperial remnant forces after the Empire's collapse following the Battle of Endor.
Moff Gideon acquired the Darksaber and sought to rebuild Imperial power using Mandalorian and Grogu-related technology.
The Imperial Security Bureau handled intelligence, surveillance, and internal security for the Empire.
Director Krennic oversaw the Death Star project until his death during the Battle of Scarif.
The ISB agent Dedra Meero pursued the Rebel network led by Cassian Andor and Luthen Rael.
Imperial propaganda portrayed the Jedi as traitors and enemies of the people.
The Empire suppressed entire cultures and species, particularly non-human populations.
Wookiees were enslaved by the Empire and used as forced labor on various construction projects.

# =====================
# LIGHTSABERS
# =====================

Lightsabers were the iconic weapons of the Jedi and Sith, powered by kyber crystals.
Each kyber crystal naturally attuned to a Force user, giving their lightsaber a unique personality.
Blue lightsabers were traditionally associated with Jedi Guardians who focused on combat.
Green lightsabers were traditionally associated with Jedi Consulars who focused on the Force.
Purple lightsabers were extremely rare and represented a balance between the light and dark side.
Mace Windu wielded one of the only known purple lightsabers in the Jedi Order.
Yellow lightsabers were used by Jedi Sentinels and Temple Guards.
Red lightsabers were created when Sith corrupted kyber crystals through the dark side in a process called bleeding.
White lightsabers were created when a corrupted crystal was purified, representing neutrality.
Black lightsabers, also known as the Darksaber, were unique artifacts of Mandalorian Jedi Tarre Vizsla.
Kyber crystals screamed in the dark side when bled, feeling pain during the process.
Ahsoka Tano reversed the bleeding process on Inquisitor kyber crystals to create her white blades.
Jedi constructed their lightsabers through a deeply personal Force-guided meditation process.
The construction of a lightsaber was considered a rite of passage for Jedi Padawans.
Dual-wielding two lightsabers was a combat style used by Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano.
The double-bladed lightsaber was favored by Darth Maul and some Inquisitors.
Crossguard lightsabers like Kylo Ren's cracked crystal created unstable plasma vents as side guards.
Form I Shii-Cho was the foundational lightsaber combat form, teaching basic attack and defense.
Form II Makashi was an elegant dueling form favored by Count Dooku, optimized for lightsaber-to-lightsaber combat.
Form III Soresu was a purely defensive form mastered by Obi-Wan Kenobi.
Form IV Ataru was an acrobatic offensive form favored by Yoda and Qui-Gon Jinn.
Form V Shien and Djem So were aggressive power-based forms focused on dominating opponents.
Anakin Skywalker was a master of Djem So and combined it with his natural aggressive instincts.
Form VI Niman was a balanced form that incorporated Force powers and various other techniques.
Form VII Juyo and Vaapad were aggressive, unpredictable forms that risked channeling the dark side.
Mace Windu invented Vaapad as a refinement of Juyo that channeled his own dark impulses outward.

Barriss Offee was Luminara Unduli's Padawan who betrayed the Jedi Order and framed Ahsoka Tano.
Barriss believed the Jedi Order had become corrupt and too militarized during the Clone Wars.
Coleman Trebor attempted to attack Count Dooku during the Battle of Geonosis and was killed by Jango Fett.
Even Piell was a Jedi Master who held half of the Nexus Route coordinates and died during the Citadel rescue.
Tera Sinube was an elderly but wise Jedi Master who assisted Ahsoka on Coruscant.
Sifo-Dyas was the Jedi Master who commissioned the clone army on his own initiative, sensing coming war.
Cal Kestis survived Order 66 as a Padawan on Bracca and later fought to restore the Jedi Order.
Cal discovered the location of Force-sensitive children who could potentially restore the Jedi Order.

Cere Junda reconnected with the Force after severing herself from it under Imperial torture.
The Bendu was an ancient Force-sensitive being who claimed to stand in the middle, serving neither light nor dark.

# =====================
# TECHNOLOGY AND SHIPS
# =====================

Hyperspace travel allowed ships to travel between star systems in hours or days rather than millennia.
Hyperdrives were the engines that allowed ships to enter and traverse hyperspace.
Astromech droids like R2-D2 calculated hyperspace coordinates and maintained starfighter systems.
The Millennium Falcon was a Corellian YT-1300 freighter considered one of the fastest ships in the galaxy.
Han Solo boasted the Falcon could make the Kessel Run in under twelve parsecs due to a dangerous shortcut.
The Falcon had a hyperdrive that frequently malfunctioned but could be remarkably fast when operational.
TIE fighters were the primary Imperial starfighter, fast but unshielded and dependent on carrier ships.
X-wings were the primary Rebel starfighter, balanced between speed, firepower, and shielding.
Y-wings were older Rebel bombers used for capital ship and ground assault missions.
A-wings were fast Rebel interceptors used to counter Imperial TIE fighters.
B-wings were heavy assault starfighters used by the Rebellion against capital ships.
Star Destroyers were the iconic warships of the Empire, a kilometer long and bristling with weapons.
Super Star Destroyers were massive flagships many times the size of standard Star Destroyers.
The Executor was Darth Vader's Super Star Destroyer that served as his personal flagship.
Mon Calamari Star Cruisers were the backbone of the Rebel Alliance's capital ship fleet.
Clone gunships were Low Altitude Assault Transports used to deliver clone troopers into battle.
AT-AT walkers were massive four-legged Imperial assault walkers used to intimidate and overwhelm ground forces.
AT-ST walkers were smaller two-legged scout walkers used by the Empire for patrol and support.
The Death Star's superlaser was powered by focusing kyber crystal energy through a series of tributaries.
Proton torpedoes were a standard starfighter munition effective against heavily armored targets.
Ion cannons could disable ships without destroying them by disrupting electronic systems.
The Holo-Net was the galaxy-wide communications network maintained by the Republic and later the Empire.
Holocommunicators allowed real-time holographic communication across interstellar distances.
Carbonite freezing was a process originally used for storing goods in deep space transport.
Boba Fett suggested using carbonite to freeze Han Solo as a test of the process for freezing Luke.
Jabba the Hutt displayed Han Solo's frozen form as a wall decoration in his Tatooine palace.
Kyber crystals were naturally connected to the Force and formed in cold environments like Ilum's caves.
The Death Star's superlaser required enormous quantities of kyber crystals to power its planet-killing shot.
Jedha City was a holy site where pilgrims came to obtain kyber crystals before it was partially destroyed.

# =====================
# THE NEW REPUBLIC AND RESISTANCE
# =====================

The New Republic was established following the defeat of the Empire at Endor.
Mon Mothma became the first Chancellor of the New Republic after leading the Rebellion.
The New Republic demilitarized significantly, preferring political solutions over military ones.
This demilitarization left the New Republic vulnerable to threats like the First Order.
The New Republic Senate relocated its capital regularly among member worlds to avoid concentrating power.
Hosnian Prime was the New Republic capital destroyed by Starkiller Base.
Leia Organa was marginalized by the New Republic Senate after Claudia Gray published her parentage.
The revelation that Leia was Darth Vader's daughter caused political damage and distrust.
Leia formed the Resistance as a private military force to monitor and counter the First Order.
The New Republic unofficially supported the Resistance with supplies and intelligence.
After Hosnian Prime's destruction, the New Republic effectively ceased to exist as a governing body.
The Resistance became the primary armed opposition to the First Order following Hosnian Prime.
The New Republic was eventually reestablished following the defeat of the First Order and Final Order at Exegol.
